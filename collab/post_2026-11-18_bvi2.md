## East-west backup to the BVI: two winters

The best pairs from Monday's screen, now on both the 2023–24 and 2024–25 winters. Rev E1, 500 voyages each, the same seeds, and an approach to the islands that keeps further south.

| Launch → BVI | Launches | Success (95% range) | 2023–24 / 2024–25 | Median days | Arrive in March | Storm within 200 km / hurricane |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Mindelo, Cape Verde** | 15 Nov–10 Jan | **86.0%** (83.0–89.0) | 84.8 / 87.2 | 93 | **51%** | 0.4% / 0.2% |
| El Hierro, via 20N 28W | 1 Nov–20 Dec | 81.0% (77.4–84.2) | 78.0 / 84.0 | 134 | 38% | 0.4% / 0.1% |
| Gran Canaria, via 20N 28W | 1 Nov–20 Dec | 78.0% (74.2–81.6) | 74.0 / 82.0 | 136 | 37% | 0.3% / 0.0% |
| Gran Canaria, via 20N 28W, **May** (Oshen's timing) | May 2024, May 2025 | 82.6% (79.2–86.0) | 81.6 / 83.6 | 105 | 0% (Aug–Sep) | **19.6% / 10.5%** |

- **Cape Verde holds up across both winters:** +8.0 points over Gran Canaria (+3.6 to +12.4) and +5.0 over El Hierro (+0.6 to +9.4), on the same seeds. The boats that arrived in March launched between 23 November and 4 January (median 14 December).
- **The Canaries come out slower over two winters** than Monday's one-winter screen showed, about 135 days. 2023–24 was the harder winter.
- **May launches** score about the same before storms are counted, but 1 in 5 passes within 200 km of a tropical storm and 1 in 10 near a hurricane, arriving at the peak of the season. With our rough storm loss model they drop to about 74%.
- **The southern approach to the islands helped:** passes within 1 km of the rocks off Virgin Gorda fell from about 10% to 3%, and Anegada's reef is about 4%. About two thirds of boats reach the holding point south of Norman and Peter Islands.
- **Our recommendation to Randolph** for a March arrival: launch from Mindelo between about 25 November and 5 January. The fallback is El Hierro or Gran Canaria routed via 20N 28W, launching mid-November to mid-December.

- Holding offshore gets many more boats to the pickup point, and almost none pass close to the Manacles or the Lizard.
- Approaching the coast at all still costs 4–6 points against stopping 40 km out. In our model that's mainly the higher collision and capture rates near land.
- Our recommendation to Randolph: the Lizard offing as the holding point, with the mouth of Mount's Bay as the alternative. The first approach waypoint off Scilly needs to move further out, because 12–15% of boats pass within 1 km of the islands.
